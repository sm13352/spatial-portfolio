Lab 01

The question

Where are China's major cities, roads, and rivers located in relation to its administrative boundaries?

The data

Where it came from, when it was collected, who made it, and under what licence. Then the part people skip: what is missing from it.

The method

What you actually did, in enough detail that someone else could repeat it. Name the tool, the projection, and any threshold or class break you chose. If you chose a number, say why you chose that number.

What I found

Plain language. What does the map support? Write it so someone outside this course could follow it.

What this does not show

The honest part. What the map cannot support, what you had to assume, and what would change your answer. If your unit of analysis could have been drawn differently, say so here.

AI use: AI-assisted for [what], using [which tool]. I verified [how]. If you did not use AI, write "No AI tools were used for this entry."
