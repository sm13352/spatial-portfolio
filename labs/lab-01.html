Lab 01

The question

Where are China's major cities, roads, and rivers located in relation to its administrative boundaries?

The data

I used the China Layers teaching web map hosted by Esri. It includes World Countries (Generalized), China Admin, China Rivers, China Roads, and World Cities. One provenance issue is that the World Cities layer gives Chongqing a population of about 12.5 million from UN-Habitat, representing the built-up agglomeration, while the municipality has about 32 million people. The China Admin layer also contains 63 polygons even though China has 34 province-level units, because some units are represented by multiple polygons.

The method

For the Soho analysis, I used weighted kernel density estimation in ArcGIS Online. I weighted the cholera case points by Num_Cases so that addresses with more deaths had more influence on the heat map. I changed the bandwidth using the unlabelled Area of influence slider and tested three positions: far left, middle, and far right. I then compared the three maps to see how the location and size of the hottest area changed.

What I found

The sensitivity test showed that the result changed depending on the area of influence. At the far-left setting, there were several small hotspots and no clear overall centre. At the middle setting, the cases formed a clearer central hotspot, with the 43 Broadwick Street pump nearest the centre. At the far-right setting, 43 Broadwick Street was still nearest the centre, but the pattern was too smoothed to identify a clear hotspot. Therefore, the conclusion about 43 Broadwick Street was clear at two of the three settings, showing that the result is sensitive to the bandwidth.

What this does not show

The heat map does not prove that the 43 Broadwick Street pump caused the cholera outbreak. It only shows the spatial concentration of reported cases. The result also depends on the chosen area of influence: changing the bandwidth changed how clearly the centre appeared. The map may also be affected by missing or inaccurate historical records and does not show other factors that could have influenced where cholera cases occurred.
