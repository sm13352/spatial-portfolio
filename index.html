SOCS-SHU 205 · Fall 2026

Sazhida Mukanbetkaliyeva

I study social studies at NYU Shanghai. This site collects my work in spatial analytics: what I asked, what I mapped, what I found, and what I could not answer.

Labs

  • Lab 01 China in Layers and Soho Cholera Exploring China's cities, roads, rivers, and administrative boundaries, as well as the spatial pattern of cholera cases in Soho.
  • Lab 02 U.S. Elections Exploring the 2016 U.S. presidential election through different mapping techniques.
  • Lab 03 One Perimeter, Three Answers Measuring the Soberanes Fire through projection, raster resolution, and terrain.
  • Lab 04 The Same Layer, Four Cell Sizes Comparing vector and raster representations of Shanghai to examine how projection, cell size, grid origin, and spatial resolution change the results.
  • Problem Set 01 Spatial Differences in Severe Income Loss Examining whether severe income loss during the 2022 Shanghai lockdown differed between the seven central districts and the other nine districts.

Final project

My storytelling web map, due in December. I will put the question and the link here as it develops.
